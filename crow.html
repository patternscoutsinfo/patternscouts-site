Help

Questions people actually ask.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Crow opens your spreadsheet on your machine and writes the result back on your machine. The only time it contacts the internet is to check your licence and to fetch new tool versions. Your data is never uploaded anywhere.

Do I need Microsoft Excel installed?

No. Crow reads and writes .xlsx and .csv files on its own. Excel does not need to be on the machine.

How do updates work?

Crow updates itself automatically in the background when your computer starts. You will never see a popup or be asked to do anything. One day you open Crow and it quietly has new tools or improvements — that is it.

I paid but my key has not arrived.

Keys are sent by hand, usually within one business day. If it has been longer, reply to your payment email with the screenshot still attached — we will sort it the same day.

Can I move the licence to another computer?

Yes. Email us from your registered address with the new machine's details and we will move the key across. Your old machine stops using it at that point. Nothing is deleted.

My antivirus is blocking Crow. What do I do?

Add Crow.exe to your antivirus exclusion list. In Kaspersky, go to Exclusions and actions on object detection → Exclusions → Add → browse to Crow.exe → tick File or folder → Add. After that, Crow updates without interruption.

Can I request a tool that doesn't exist yet?

Yes — that is what the Request a tool button is for inside Crow. Describe the job you keep doing by hand, step by step, and it comes straight to us. We build the tools users actually need.

Windows says "Unknown publisher" when I download. Is it safe?

Yes, completely safe. Windows shows this warning for any new software that has not yet built up a download history with Microsoft — it is not a sign of a virus or problem. Click More info and then Run anyway to install. Once Crow is installed it updates itself silently in the background and you will never see this screen again.

Which versions of Windows does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.
