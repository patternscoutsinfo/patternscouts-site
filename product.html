Help

Questions people actually ask.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Crow opens your spreadsheet on your machine and writes the result back on your machine. The only time it contacts the internet is to check your licence and fetch new tool versions. Your data is never uploaded anywhere.

Do I need Microsoft Excel installed?

No. Crow reads and writes .xlsx and .csv files on its own. Excel does not need to be on the machine.

How do updates work?

Crow updates itself automatically in the background when your computer starts. You will never see a popup or be asked to do anything.

I paid but my key has not arrived.

Keys are sent by hand, usually within one business day. If it has been longer, email us with your payment screenshot and we will sort it the same day.

Can I move the licence to another computer?

Yes. Email us from your registered address with the new machine's details and we will move the key across.

My antivirus is blocking Crow. What do I do?

Add Crow.exe to your antivirus exclusion list. After that, Crow updates without interruption.

Can I request a tool that doesn't exist yet?

Yes — that is what the Request a tool button is for inside Crow. Describe the job you keep doing by hand and it comes straight to us.

Windows says "Unknown publisher" when I download. Is it safe?

Yes, completely safe. Click More info then Run anyway to install.
